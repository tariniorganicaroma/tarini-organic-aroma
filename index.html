100% Farmer Sourced & Chemical-Free

The Soul of Kerala's
Hill Tracts

Tarini Organic Aroma is rooted from a simple promise — to capture the pure essence of our spices. Our organically grown plantations in the Malabar Hills are guided through traditional methods that have been practiced for generations to nurture the most vibrant flavors of every seed and root.

Moreover, sustainability lies at our core, through approaches like natural composting and bio-diverse farming practices, each spice carries not just robust flavors but also serves as a wholesome, pure and a genuine bridge between heritage and home.

Spices of Wayanad

Nestled high in the Western Ghats, Wayanad is Kerala's legendary spice garden. The unique combination of mist-clad mountains, fertile forest soil, rich biodiversity, and high rainfall creates the perfect cradle for nurturing some of the world's most aromatic and therapeutic spices.

At Tarini, we work hand-in-hand with native farming families to bring you hand-selected, sun-dried spices that capture the true, unaltered soul of Wayanad. No preservatives, no additives—just pure, unadulterated freshness.

Mist-Clad Terroir

Grown at high altitudes in pristine forest soils.
